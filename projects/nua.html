01 — Objective

Diagnosing the hesitation

The brief: understand why potential customers hesitate to switch to or try Nua, and identify the highest-impact levers to drive first-time adoption in a category shaped heavily by habit.

Primary research at scale

Conducted 116 surveys and 40 interviews to map decision drivers, habit inertia and switching triggers in the menstrual hygiene category.

The research went beyond stated preference to understand the real friction between intent and first purchase.
