01 — The Idea

Bottling India's most loved chaat flavour

Lahori Panipuri is a mass Indian beverage concept that bottles the iconic teekha-khatta taste of panipuri into a refreshing carbonated drink.

Rooted in Indian street culture and priced for everyday consumption, it is built for scale across kirana stores and quick-commerce — making a beloved street flavour accessible, affordable and habitual, one bottle at a time.

One flavour, three SKUs

The lineup mirrors how India already consumes panipuri: a ₹10 / 250ml hero SKU for mass trial and kirana penetration, a ₹20 / 500ml pack for repeat home consumption, and a ₹180 pack of 20 built for panipuri vendors, chaat shops and small eateries.

Each SKU is mapped to a specific region and consumption behaviour — designed for habit formation at scale.

03 — Go-to-Market

Win the street, win the shelf, win the area

The marketing strategy — campaign-named "Har Gali Panipuri" — is built on a simple belief: in mass India, brands are made at the kirana counter, not on billboards.

It works street by street: free tasting and first-stock for shopkeepers, loud unmistakable packaging acting as a shelf billboard, and panipuri vendors driving word of mouth. Once repeat orders start, the brand expands to adjacent streets for area-level dominance.

Measured from day one

The strategy includes a full launch dashboard — tracking Active Outlet Penetration as the north-star metric, alongside numeric distribution, rate of sale, repeat rate and unit economics.

Clear launch gates on distribution, velocity and repeat unlock each new geography and spend tier — scaling on evidence, not assumption.
