About

Anisha builds brands the hard way — then makes them simple.

She founded and scaled EKDOR, a vertically integrated D2C women's apparel brand in the niche craft category of Chikankari — taking it past ₹1Cr in revenue with healthy margins and a loyal, repeat-led customer base.

That meant doing all of it: managing an unorganised multi-vendor supply chain, designing a hybrid logistics network, running performance marketing at a sustained ~4x ROAS, building an 85K-strong community, and hiring and training a cross-functional team of eight.

Now a PGP in Startup Leadership candidate at Mesa School of Business (top 5 percentile), she brings that full-stack operator's perspective to strategy for consumer brands — across FemTech, fragrance, sustainable goods and more. Her work pairs primary customer research with execution thinking: the view of someone who has actually built and run a brand, not only advised one.
